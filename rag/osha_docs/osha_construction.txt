OSHA CONSTRUCTION SAFETY STANDARDS

STANDARD 1926.20 - GENERAL SAFETY AND HEALTH PROVISIONS

All construction work must be performed in a manner that does not expose 
workers to safety or health hazards.

Site Safety Requirements:
- Safety vests required in high visibility areas
- Hard hats required at all construction sites
- Safety boots required where there is danger of foot injuries
- Gloves required when handling sharp materials

STANDARD 1926.28 - PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

The employer is responsible for requiring the wearing of appropriate 
personal protective equipment in all operations where there is exposure 
to hazardous conditions.

CONSTRUCTION SITE VIOLATIONS - MOST COMMON:
1. No hard hat: $15,625 fine per worker
2. No safety vest in traffic areas: $15,625 fine
3. No fall protection: $15,625 to $156,259
4. No eye protection during grinding: $15,625

SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION:
- Critical: Worker in immediate danger, stop work order issued
- High: Clear PPE violation visible, immediate correction required
- Medium: PPE present but worn incorrectly
- Low: Minor deviation from standards, verbal warning sufficient

COMPLIANCE SCORING:
- 90-100: Excellent - Full PPE compliance observed
- 70-89: Good - Minor issues detected
- 50-69: Fair - Multiple violations present
- Below 50: Poor - Immediate intervention required
