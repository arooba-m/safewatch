
OSHA PENALTY STRUCTURE 2024-2025

SERIOUS VIOLATIONS
Definition: Substantial probability that death or serious physical harm could result
Penalty: Up to $15,625 per violation

WILLFUL VIOLATIONS  
Definition: Employer knowingly disregards OSHA requirements
Penalty: $11,524 to $156,259 per violation

REPEAT VIOLATIONS
Definition: Same standard violated within 5 years
Penalty: Up to $156,259 per violation

OTHER THAN SERIOUS
Definition: Violation with no direct relationship to safety/health
Penalty: Up to $15,625 per violation

PPE SPECIFIC FINES:
- No hard hat on construction site: $15,625 (serious)
- No safety vest in required area: $15,625 (serious)  
- No eye protection: $15,625 (serious)
- No fall protection: $156,259 (willful)
- Repeat PPE violation: $156,259

REDUCTION FACTORS:
- Good faith effort: up to 25% reduction
- Small business: up to 60% reduction
- History of compliance: up to 10% reduction

OSHA INSPECTION TRIGGERS:
- Employee complaint
- Fatality or hospitalization report
- Referral from other agencies
- Planned high-hazard industry inspection
- Follow-up inspection
