OSHA STANDARD 1926.100 - HEAD PROTECTION

Requirements:
Employees working in areas where there is a possible danger of head injury from 
impact, falling or flying objects must be protected by protective helmets.

Hard hat classes:
- Class A: General service, limited voltage protection
- Class B: Utility service, high voltage protection  
- Class C: Special service, no voltage protection

When required:
- Construction sites with overhead work
- Areas where objects might fall from above
- Work near exposed electrical conductors

Fine for violation: $15,625 per serious violation
Repeat violation fine: $156,259

OSHA STANDARD 1926.102 - EYE AND FACE PROTECTION

Requirements:
Eye and face protection must be provided when machines or operations present 
potential eye or face injury from physical, chemical, or radiation agents.

Types required:
- Safety glasses for flying debris
- Goggles for chemical splash
- Face shields for grinding operations

Fine for violation: $15,625 per violation

---

OSHA STANDARD 1910.132 - PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT GENERAL

Requirements:
Protective equipment must be provided, used, and maintained in a sanitary 
and reliable condition wherever it is necessary.

Employer responsibilities:
- Assess workplace for hazards
- Select appropriate PPE
- Train employees on PPE use
- Enforce PPE compliance

Fine for willful violation: $156,259
Fine for serious violation: $15,625
Fine for other than serious: $15,625
